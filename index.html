How It Works

This calculator estimates the total combat XP budget a party can handle between two long rests.

Daily budgets are based on the moderate encounter XP thresholds from D&D 2024. Easy adventuring days use two moderate encounter budgets, Medium days use three, and Hard days use four.

Short rests adjust the daily budget. One short rest applies a 0.90 multiplier, two use 1.00, three use 1.10, and four or more use 1.15.

Add encounters to subtract their XP values from the available budget. You can rename encounters or edit their XP at any time.

This is an unofficial homebrew planning tool. Actual encounter difficulty depends on party composition, tactics, monster abilities and available resources. Daily XP budgets do not replace individual encounter difficulty guidelines.
